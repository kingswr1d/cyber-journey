# resources

every free / paid resource i use, with honest notes.

## cert study

- **professor messer security+** — youtube + his site, ~$30 for practice exams. free study videos are the gold standard. https://www.professormesser.com/
- **examcompass security+** — free practice questions, decent. https://www.examcompass.com/
- **darril gibson "get and build" book** — $15 ebook. read the plugin if messer videos aren't enough on their own.

## labs (the core)

- **tryhackme** — beginner-friendly, browser-based, guided paths. free tier covers SOC L1 path fully. https://tryhackme.com
- **letsdefend** — SOC simulator with realistic alerts + investigations. free tier is limited; $30/mo unlocks everything. https://letsdefend.io
- **hackthebox** — only after i finish tryhackme SOC L1 path. harder, more realistic, less guidance.
- **proxmox + security onion** — only IF i upgrade access or use a different machine. not feasible on 5gb ram.

## siem tools (week 7+)

- **wazuh** — open source, free, runs on limited ram (5gb doable with single-node + no elastic). https://wazuh.com
- **elastic security** — free, but heavy. skip on this laptop.
- **security onion** — comprehensive but 16gb+. save for year 2 with a proper homelab.

## job hunt (week 10+)

- **pramp** — free mock interviews. https://pramp.com
- **linkedin premium** — $40/mo. worth it for 1 month in week 10 to send SOEs.
- **resume.io / rxresu.me** — resume builders, free tier works.

## community

- **r/cybersecurity, r/socanalyst** — read-only at the start. ask questions from week 4+.
- **cyberseclabs.co.uk discord, hackthebox discord, letsdefend discord** — most active security learning communities.
- **linkedin** — find SOC analysts at target companies, ask for 15-min coffee chats in week 9+.

## what I will NOT buy

- "udemy courses" — most are AI-generated or outdated. free youtube is better for everything i need.
- "bootcamps" — $5k+ for what's free on tryhackme.
- "cert bundles" — professor messer + practice exams is all you need for security+.

## decisions to make later

- wazuh vs security onion — depends on hardware access by week 7.
